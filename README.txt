Lemma home screen app
=====================

These files are the complete app. Put them, all in one folder, on any website that uses https
(GitHub Pages is free). Then open that address in Safari on your iPad, tap Share, and choose
Add to Home Screen.

Files: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, math.js, tex-svg.js, transformers.min.js,
ort-wasm-simd-threaded.jsep.mjs, ort-wasm-simd-threaded.jsep.wasm, icon-180.png, icon-192.png,
icon-512.png, icon-1024.png

Check, LaTeX, Practice and graphing from your working run on the iPad. The first time you use one,
Lemma downloads its maths reader (about 80 MB). The reader is Texo by Sicheng Mao, open source under
the AGPL-3.0 licence (github.com/alephpi/Texo).

Your notebooks are stored on the iPad, not on the website. Use More > Back up all notebooks
to keep a copy in Files.
