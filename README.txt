Lemma home screen app
=====================

These files are the complete app. Put them, all in one folder, on any website that uses https
(GitHub Pages is free). Then open that address in Safari on your iPad, tap Share, and choose
Add to Home Screen.

Files: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, math.js, tex-svg.js, icon-180.png, icon-192.png,
icon-512.png, icon-1024.png

Your notebooks are stored on the iPad, not on the website. Use More > Back up all notebooks
to keep a copy in Files.
